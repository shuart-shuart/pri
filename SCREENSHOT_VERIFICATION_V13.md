The four user-supplied screenshots of the original About and instruction detail screens are now the principal source for page composition.

- Fixed 49px desktop top bar and 50px fixed footer, with light divider lines.
- About intro starts close to the left gutter and is approximately 75% wide; subsequent body content moves to a narrow column beginning around 38% of the viewport width.
- Team heading remains on the left, profiles sit in the narrow right column, and acknowledgements remain near the end.
- Individual instruction top bar contains number, title, artist and search.
- Instruction text now reads as one inline passage with grey Go to / at / and connective words; lower region has map placeholder, source contextual writing, and tags.
- The original screenshots show actual map imagery and SAW/NAC logos. Their graphic assets are not yet established in the reconstruction, so the map is represented by a neutral placeholder and sponsor names by text, not invented logos.
- Content and instruction numbering follow the currently recovered archive, which may differ from the specific screenshot example.
- On smaller screens the desktop columns stack into a single readable column and footer links remain keyboard accessible.

These are screenshot-grounded CSS approximations and have not been certified pixel-perfect across browsers.
