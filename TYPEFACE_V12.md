- All site pages request the publicly hosted Font Library stylesheet `https://fontlibrary.org//face/standard`.
- `standard-typeface.css` uses its named Book and Bold faces in the reconstructed website.
- Font binaries are not included; online access is necessary to obtain the webfont, with Arial/Helvetica fallback offline.
- The homepage instruction text, spacing and controls from Version 11 are otherwise untouched.
- The map remains a 100vw × 94vh grey placeholder. The archive did not include the marker database.
- The Resources page remains absent.
